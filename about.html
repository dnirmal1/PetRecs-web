About Us

Our Mission

Empowering Pet Healthcare Through Technology

At PetRecs, we believe that exceptional pet healthcare begins with seamless communication and efficient practice management. Our mission is to empower veterinary clinics and pet parents with innovative technology that simplifies care coordination, strengthens relationships, and ultimately improves outcomes for pets.

We're committed to transforming the pet healthcare industry by providing a comprehensive, dual-platform solution that addresses the unique challenges faced by both veterinary professionals and pet owners in today's digital age.

Dual-Platform Solution

One Ecosystem, Two Powerful Platforms

For Veterinary Clinics

Comprehensive practice management software designed specifically for the unique needs of veterinary professionals.

  • Digital appointment scheduling and calendar management
  • Electronic health records (EHR) with medical history tracking
  • Automated reminders for vaccines and checkups
  • Inventory and prescription management
  • Billing and invoicing integration
  • Staff collaboration and workflow tools
  • Analytics and reporting dashboard
  • HIPAA-compliant data security

For Pet Parents

User-friendly mobile and web app that keeps pet owners connected, informed, and engaged with their pet's healthcare.

  • Easy appointment booking and rescheduling
  • Access to complete pet health records anytime
  • Automatic vaccination and appointment reminders
  • Direct messaging with veterinary team
  • Prescription refill requests
  • Educational resources and pet care tips
  • Multiple pet profile management
  • Secure payment and billing access
